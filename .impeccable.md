## Design Context

### Users
- **Educational Administrators**: Managing institutional data and assets.
- **Teachers**: Making pedagogical reservations and borrowing equipment.
- **Staff/Maintenance**: Tracking inventory and reporting incidents.
- **Context**: Used in busy educational environments where efficiency and clarity are paramount.

### Brand Personality
- **Voice**: Professional, utilitarian, and reliable.
- **Tone**: "Fast and dense and unimpressed" - prioritizing information over decoration.
- **3-word personality**: Utilitarian, Flat, Precise.
- **Emotional goals**: Confidence in data accuracy and speed of operation.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual tone**: "Jira-inspired Flat" - No shadows, crisp 1px borders, tight corners (radius: 0.375rem).
- **References**: Atlassian Design System, Linear, GitHub.
- **Anti-references**: "AI Slop" (no neon gradients, no floating glass cards, no heavy rounded corners).
- **Theme**: Native support for Light and Dark modes using OKLCH perceptually uniform colors.

### Design Principles
1. **Utility First**: Every pixel must serve a functional purpose.
2. **High Density**: Optimize for expert users who need to see a lot of data at once.
3. **Flatness**: Maintain a single plane; use borders and background tints for separation instead of elevation.
4. **Predictability**: Consistent interactions across all modules (Inventory, Loans, etc.).
5. **Mobile-First Flow**: Complex desktop interactions must have a dedicated, simplified flow for mobile devices.
